## Interview Question

When do you use for, foreach, or while?

## What The Interviewer Is Testing

Whether you choose loops based on the problem, not habit.

## A Strong Answer Should Cover

- `foreach` is the default for walking over every item of an array.
- `for` fits a known number of repetitions controlled by a counter.
- `while` fits repeating until a condition changes, such as reading until the end of a file.

## Practice Task

Give one realistic example for each loop and explain the choice.
